# Code of conduct

We want Cofound Investment Office to be a respectful, useful and safe project.
Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate professionally,
assume good faith, welcome constructive disagreement and avoid harassment,
discrimination, intimidation or disclosure of another person's private data.

Investment documents are especially sensitive. Never post a real BP, company
identity, personal information, credential, private connector locator or
confidential analysis in a public Issue, discussion or pull request.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict
participation or report serious abuse when conduct threatens people, data or
the project. Report sensitive conduct concerns through the private channel
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
