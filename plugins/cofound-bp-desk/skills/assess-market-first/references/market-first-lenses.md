# Market-first seven lenses

This configurable lens is informed by the user-supplied summary of Sequoia-related industry discussion and the linked article below. It is not an official or proprietary Sequoia scorecard, and the article was not independently verified during packaging.

User-supplied reference: <https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/XmG6ic4dI1WNVl-Ny_pYcw>

1. **Market position:** market ceiling, likely inflection window, value-chain position, substitution risk and policy sensitivity.
2. **Founder fit:** industry insight, relevant 0-to-1 execution evidence, recruiting ability and downside preparation.
3. **Metric evidence:** sector- and stage-appropriate traction, retention, unit economics and data-definition consistency.
4. **Business-model resilience:** incumbent copying, demand contraction, acquisition-cost inflation, supply constraints and margin pressure.
5. **Term compatibility:** financing terms that may impair founder incentives, control, employee equity or later fundraising.
6. **Investor support fit:** customer, talent, industry and crisis support the investor can realistically provide.
7. **Exit hypothesis:** strategic buyers, future financing and capital-market feasibility, treated as hypotheses rather than forecasts.

Market excitement cannot substitute for customer evidence. Conversely, limited early revenue is not automatically negative when the project is still at technical validation stage; state what proof is appropriate for the actual round.
