# BP optimization framework

Use this reference after the project evidence and investment analysis have been loaded.

## Decision-first narrative

Treat the deck as a sequence of investment questions rather than a fixed visual template:

| Decision question | Typical evidence | Common failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What changed, and why now? | regulation, cost curve, technology shift, buyer behavior | generic macro trend without a timing mechanism |
| Whose problem is urgent? | named buyer, workflow, current cost, frequency | broad persona with no budget owner |
| Why does this solution win? | product workflow, measured outcome, deployment proof | feature list without a differentiated result |
| Is there commercial proof? | paid orders, revenue, repeat use, delivery and collection | mixing LOI, contract value, delivered revenue and cash received |
| Can the model scale? | gross margin logic, sales cycle, unit economics, capacity | extrapolating from one customer without constraints |
| Is the market worth entering? | bottom-up customer count, spend, adoption path, competition | unsupported top-down TAM |
| Can this team execute? | relevant achievements, division of responsibility, hiring gaps | biographies without evidence of execution |
| What does this round prove? | amount, use of funds, milestones, runway | expense list with no proof milestone |

Merge questions when evidence is light. Split a question only when one page cannot support a single clear decision.

## Page quality rules

- Give each page one decision and one declarative headline.
- Put the strongest verified evidence near the claim it supports.
- Use charts for comparisons or change over time, tables for exact mappings, and diagrams for systems or flows.
- Label units, currencies, time ranges, cohorts, data sources, and whether a number is contracted, delivered, invoiced, collected, or forecast.
- Prefer a small number of readable facts over dense collections of unsupported statements.
- Use neutral placeholders such as `待补证据` instead of fabricated content.

## Suggested visual system

- Use a restrained financial-document palette with one signal color.
- Establish clear display, section, body, caption, and evidence-note levels.
- Keep repeated navigation, page number, confidentiality mark, and source notes consistent.
- Do not shrink critical text to preserve a crowded layout; reduce content or split the page.
- Avoid decorative stock imagery, generic AI gradients, excessive cards, and charts that overstate precision.

## Final QA

Before delivering an edited copy or a production brief, check:

1. every factual claim against the evidence ledger;
2. all amounts, currencies, dates, percentages, and period labels;
3. consistency between orders, revenue, delivery, invoices, and cash collection;
4. consistency between financing amount, use of funds, burn, runway, and milestones;
5. page order, page numbers, citations, chart labels, and confidentiality text;
6. rendered pages for clipping, overlap, unreadable type, distorted images, or low contrast;
7. that the original file remains unchanged.
